Hermes Mail Digest

A single-user mail briefing agent, run privately by its owner.

What this app does

Hermes Mail Digest is a personal automation that reads its owner's own Gmail inbox and produces a short daily briefing: the messages that actually need a reply, an action, or a deadline, with everything else filtered out. The briefing is delivered to the owner's private chat channel.

How it works

  • Access is granted by the owner through Google OAuth, for the owner's account only.
  • It runs on the owner's own private server. There is no hosted service and no multi-tenant platform.
  • It requests read access to Gmail, plus Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts, all of which are limited to the owner's own account and data.
  • It reads mail. It does not send, delete, archive, or modify anything unless the owner asks it to.
  • No data is shared with, sold to, or processed by any third party, and none is used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page.

Who uses it

One person: the account owner. This is a personal tool, not a product offered to the public, so there is no sign-up, no user list and no service to subscribe to.
